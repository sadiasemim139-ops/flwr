About Petal Studio

Celebrating Flowers in Every Form

Petal Studio is dedicated to showcasing the full spectrum of floral beauty — from classical botanical paintings to modern digital edits and the cutting-edge world of AI-generated art.

We believe flowers represent something universal: growth, beauty, and the passage of time. By exploring flowers through art, editing, and AI, we discover new dimensions of this timeless subject.
